# Glossary

Every term used anywhere in these notes. Start with the bolded ones.

## Start with these

- **[[Put Credit Spread]]** — The bot's main trade: sell a put, buy a cheaper one as insurance.
- **[[Max Loss]]** — The worst thing that can happen on a trade.
- **[[Delta]]** — Roughly, the chance an option finishes in the money.
- **[[IV Rank]]** — Where today's volatility sits compared to the past year.
- **[[The 1% Rule]]** — Never risk more than 1% of the account on one trade.
- **[[Expectancy]]** — Average profit per trade, wins and losses combined.

## Everything else

- [[Assignment]] — Being forced to actually buy the shares.
- [[Backtest]] — Replaying the strategy over past market data.
- [[Bid-Ask Spread]] — The difference between the buy price and the sell price.
- [[Call]] — A bet that the price goes up.
- [[Commission]] — The broker's flat fee per contract.
- [[Credit]] — Money you receive when opening a trade.
- [[DTE]] — Days To Expiration — how many days until the contract dies.
- [[Debit]] — Money you pay when opening a trade.
- [[Drawdown]] — How far the account has fallen from its best point.
- [[Expiration]] — The date the contract dies.
- [[Gamma]] — How quickly your risk changes when the stock moves.
- [[Implied Volatility]] — How much movement the market is pricing in.
- [[Iron Condor]] — A put spread and a call spread at once.
- [[Liquidity]] — Whether enough people are trading something to get a fair price.
- [[Open Interest]] — How many contracts exist right now.
- [[Option]] — A contract to buy or sell a stock at a fixed price, by a fixed date.
- [[Overfitting]] — Tuning a strategy until it perfectly fits the past.
- [[Paper Trading]] — Trading with fake money on a real broker connection.
- [[Portfolio Heat]] — Total risk across every open position at once.
- [[Position Sizing]] — Deciding how many contracts to trade.
- [[Premium]] — The money paid for an option.
- [[Profit Target]] — Closing early once most of the money is made.
- [[Put]] — A bet that the price goes down.
- [[Slippage]] — The gap between the price you see and the price you get.
- [[Spread]] — Two options traded together as one package.
- [[Strike]] — The fixed price written into the contract.
- [[Theta]] — How much value an option loses each day just from time passing.
- [[Time Stop]] — Closing at 21 days left, whatever is happening.
- [[Vega]] — How much an option's price moves when volatility changes.
- [[Walk-Forward Testing]] — Tuning on old data, then testing on data never seen.
- [[Width]] — The gap between the two strikes in a spread.
- [[Win Rate]] — The percentage of trades that make money.
