# Bid-Ask Spread

> [!info] In one line
> The difference between the buy price and the sell price.

Bid $0.65, ask $0.70 means you sell at 65 and buy at 70. That nickel is a cost, every time, in both directions.

It's why the bot only trades heavily-traded ETFs — thin markets have wide gaps that quietly eat the profit.
