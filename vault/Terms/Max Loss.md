# Max Loss

> [!info] In one line
> The worst thing that can happen on a trade.

For a credit spread: `(width x 100) - credit`. Sell a 3-wide for $66 and the worst case is $234.

This number is known **before** entering, which is what lets the bot size every position so a total loss costs no more than 1% of the account.
