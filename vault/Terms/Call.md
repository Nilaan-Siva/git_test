# Call

> [!info] In one line
> A bet that the price goes up.

A call option gives its owner the right to *buy* shares at a set price. People buy calls when they think a stock will rise.

This bot doesn't buy calls. It only sells them as part of an [[Iron Condor]], which is currently switched off.
