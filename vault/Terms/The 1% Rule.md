# The 1% Rule

> [!info] In one line
> Never risk more than 1% of the account on one trade.

Your rule, and the single most important line in the whole system.

On $25,000 that is **$250**. The bot works out the worst case of a trade first, and only then decides how many contracts it can buy without exceeding that. If it can't afford even one, it doesn't trade.

Why it matters: risking 1% means twenty losses in a row costs you about 18% of the account. Risking 10% means the same streak wipes you out.
