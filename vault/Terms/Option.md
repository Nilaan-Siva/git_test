# Option

> [!info] In one line
> A contract to buy or sell a stock at a fixed price, by a fixed date.

An option is a contract. It gives someone the right to buy or sell 100 shares of a stock at an agreed price, any time before an agreed date.

Two things matter immediately. First, one option always covers **100 shares** — so a price quoted as $0.68 actually costs $68. Second, there are two people in every contract: a buyer who pays, and a **seller** who gets paid and takes on an obligation. This bot is almost always the seller.
