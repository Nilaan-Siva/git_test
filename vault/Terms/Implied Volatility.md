# Implied Volatility

> [!info] In one line
> How much movement the market is pricing in.

Usually shortened to **IV**. It's the market's forecast of how much a stock will swing around.

High IV means options are expensive, because everyone expects turbulence. Low IV means they're cheap. Since the bot sells options, it wants to sell when they're expensive.
