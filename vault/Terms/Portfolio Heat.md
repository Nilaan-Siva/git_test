# Portfolio Heat

> [!info] In one line
> Total risk across every open position at once.

One trade at 1% is fine. Ten trades at 1% each is 10% at risk simultaneously — and in a crash they tend to lose together.

So there's a second cap: **6% total**, which allows six positions at once. It tightens to 4% when markets are turbulent.
