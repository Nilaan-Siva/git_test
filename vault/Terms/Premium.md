# Premium

> [!info] In one line
> The money paid for an option.

The price of the contract. When you **buy**, you pay premium. When you **sell**, you receive it.

The bot is a premium *seller*. Money lands in the account the moment a trade opens. The job from then on is keeping as much of it as possible.
