# Win Rate

> [!info] In one line
> The percentage of trades that make money.

Selling 30-delta spreads should win around **70%** of the time.

But on its own it proves nothing. A high win rate with a few catastrophic losses is a losing strategy wearing a disguise. Always read it next to [[Expectancy]].
