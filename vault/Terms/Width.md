# Width

> [!info] In one line
> The gap between the two strikes in a spread.

Sell the 537 and buy the 534 and the width is 3 points — a '3-wide' spread.

Width sets everything: a wider spread collects more money but risks more. Width x 100 is the most that can ever be at stake.

**The counter-intuitive part:** narrow spreads are *not* the safe choice for a small account. Commissions are a flat fee, so a 1-wide spread hands about 12% of every winner to the broker, while a 3-wide hands over 4%.
