# DTE

> [!info] In one line
> Days To Expiration — how many days until the contract dies.

Just a countdown. '45 DTE' means 45 days left.

The bot opens trades at **30–50 DTE** and closes them by **21 DTE** at the latest. The reason: time decay is steady and predictable in that middle stretch, and gets violent and unpredictable in the last three weeks.
