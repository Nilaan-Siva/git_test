# Commission

> [!info] In one line
> The broker's flat fee per contract.

IBKR charges **$0.65 per contract per leg**. A two-leg spread costs $1.30 to open and $1.30 to close — **$2.60 round trip**.

Flat fees hit small trades hardest. Against a $22 credit that's 12%. Against a $66 credit it's 4%. This single fact is why the bot trades 3-wide spreads and why tiny accounts struggle.
