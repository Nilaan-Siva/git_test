# Assignment

> [!info] In one line
> Being forced to actually buy the shares.

If a sold put finishes below its strike, the buyer can make you buy 100 shares at that price — potentially tens of thousands of dollars you may not have.

The insurance leg in a spread caps the damage, and the bot closes positions well before expiry to avoid the situation entirely.
