# Theta

> [!info] In one line
> How much value an option loses each day just from time passing.

Options are melting ice cubes. Every day that passes, they're worth slightly less, even if the stock doesn't move at all.

Terrible if you own one. Excellent if you **sold** one — which is why this bot sells. Theta is the wind at its back.
