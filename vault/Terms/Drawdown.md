# Drawdown

> [!info] In one line
> How far the account has fallen from its best point.

Peak $26,000, now $24,000 — that's a 7.7% drawdown.

The bot stops trading entirely at **15%**. Drawdown, not total return, is what actually ends most trading accounts, because people quit at the bottom.
