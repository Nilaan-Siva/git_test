# Liquidity

> [!info] In one line
> Whether enough people are trading something to get a fair price.

A liquid option has many buyers and sellers, so you can get in and out near the quoted price. An illiquid one is a trap: easy to enter, expensive to escape — and you always have to escape eventually.
