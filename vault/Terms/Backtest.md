# Backtest

> [!info] In one line
> Replaying the strategy over past market data.

Feed the bot real historical prices and see what it would have done.

Useful for catching a broken strategy. **Not** proof of a working one — it's easy to accidentally tune a strategy until it fits the past perfectly and fails on everything else. That's what [[Walk-Forward Testing]] is for.
