# Credit

> [!info] In one line
> Money you receive when opening a trade.

If a trade pays you to enter it, that payment is the credit. The bot's whole strategy is built on collecting credits.

The catch: the credit is your **maximum possible profit**. You can never make more than what you were paid up front.
