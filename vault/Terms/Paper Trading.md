# Paper Trading

> [!info] In one line
> Trading with fake money on a real broker connection.

A full simulation: real prices, real order handling, real delays and rejections — imaginary money.

Catches everything a backtest can't: connection drops, orders that don't fill, strange behaviour in fast markets.

**Set the paper balance to whatever you'll really fund.** Paper at $25,000 then going live at $500 makes every result meaningless, because position sizes and rounding behave completely differently.
