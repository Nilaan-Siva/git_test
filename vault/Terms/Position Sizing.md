# Position Sizing

> [!info] In one line
> Deciding how many contracts to trade.

Not a guess. It's division:

`contracts = (account x 1%) / max loss per contract`

$25,000 x 1% = $250. A trade risking $234 per contract means exactly **one** contract. $250 divided by $234 rounds down to 1.

Rounding down always. Rounding up would break the 1% rule.
