# Spread

> [!info] In one line
> Two options traded together as one package.

A spread means buying one option and selling another at the same time. The bought one acts as insurance on the sold one.

This is what makes the risk **knowable in advance**. Selling an option alone can lose an almost unlimited amount. Inside a spread, the worst case is a fixed number you know before you enter.
