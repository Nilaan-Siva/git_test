# Overfitting

> [!info] In one line
> Tuning a strategy until it perfectly fits the past.

The most common way trading systems fail. Adjust enough settings and any strategy looks brilliant on history — and then loses money immediately on live markets, because it learned that specific past rather than anything general.

The defence is refusing to judge a strategy on data used to build it.
