# Gamma

> [!info] In one line
> How quickly your risk changes when the stock moves.

The technical name for why the last weeks are dangerous. Close to expiry, a small move in the stock causes a large, fast change in the option's value.

You don't need to calculate it. Just know it's the reason for the [[Time Stop]].
