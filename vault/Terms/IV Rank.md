# IV Rank

> [!info] In one line
> Where today's volatility sits compared to the past year.

IV on its own tells you little — 20% is high for one stock and low for another. IV Rank fixes that by scoring today against the same stock's own past year, from 0 to 100.

**IV Rank 80** means options are pricier than on 80% of days in the last year. The bot only sells above **30**.

This one filter rejected 91 of 164 days in the last backtest — more than every other reason combined. It is the strategy's actual edge, and the thing most worth *not* loosening to get more trades.
