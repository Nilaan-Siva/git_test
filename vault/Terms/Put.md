# Put

> [!info] In one line
> A bet that the price goes down.

A put option gives its owner the right to *sell* shares at a set price. People buy puts as insurance against a crash, or to bet on a fall.

The bot **sells** puts. It is effectively selling that insurance and collecting the fee — which works out as long as the crash doesn't happen.
