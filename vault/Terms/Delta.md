# Delta

> [!info] In one line
> Roughly, the chance an option finishes in the money.

Delta is a decimal between 0 and 1. Treat it as a probability: a 0.30 delta put has roughly a **30% chance** of being worth something at expiry — so a 70% chance of expiring worthless, which is what the seller wants.

The bot targets 0.30. Lower would be safer but pay too little; higher pays more but loses too often.
